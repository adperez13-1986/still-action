# Round 2: the translator

28 Sep 2026. Read 1-balancer, 1-verifier, 1-claude.

## A pair worth ~one rank on a heavy, ~0 on packs: what makes it felt in a crowd

Accepted. Overkill eats the x2 in a pack, so the number can't be felt there. Two things can:

- **The mastery half** (balancer 4): an auto-set state is up on every body, so its payer pays in packs.
  In a crowd the felt pair is **mastery + payer**. The mastery pick screen names the worn parts it
  feeds ("feeds Scrap Cleaver").
- **The consume, loud but rare.** The pay effect plays only on a body that survives, or on a pushed
  pay. A kill has its own burst; a second flash on a dead body is noise. Heavies are where it lands hard.

## "The state's real job is to say when to push": agree, and it moves the HUD

A state is a *when* (rule 4). So the **rim glyph survives, changed**: from a pair badge to the push cue.

- A worn pair shows its glyph dim on the payer's top rim (loadout at a glance).
- It **lights only when a push would pay**: the payer is cooling but pushable (or ready), and the
  state is live on an enemy it `reaches`. The pushed aim prefers that body, after the threat rule.
- One cue per button: the break hint wins, but breaks are rare now, so this becomes the main push cue.
- A one-time caption, like `BREAK_CAPTION`: **"hold · pay it"**.
- Drop the setter's hollow glyph: it tells the thumb nothing.

## Verifier: "a state set from the payer's own slot pays nothing": agree

It's what a player already assumes ("two parts"). A part hitting its own state plays no pay effect, so
no flash without the damage. Mastery-set states (`by: hand | eye`) pay any slot.

## Verifier: "clumped carries no multiplier": persuaded

I had "hits harder". Better, with no number: **"On a hauled pack: catches every body in it"** (no
falloff). Bodies you can count. I keep *hauled* and the floor cinch: "pulled by you" is a moment he sees;
"2+ within 2 u" is a test he can't.
