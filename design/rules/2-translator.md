# Round 2: the translator

28 Sep 2026. Read 1-balancer, 1-verifier, 1-claude.

## The free autos: three fixes, in the hand

He confirmed the hand and eye as the only autos, and the ember shatter on the beat is close's best
moment. The fix should cost them *some* breaks, not their identity.

**(a) Mine, the autos never break.** The balancer's numbers sink it: each hulk gets 1-2 windups
before the pack dies, so close goes from 0 slams a pack to 2-3. Pass 2's "it still feels like
kiting" comes back, and the shatter goes. **Withdrawn.**

**(b) Balancer, 3 s poise per enemy.** In the hand: the first slam shatters, as now. That enemy's
next windup within 3 s lands unless you step out or push. That is exactly when a push should be
wanted: just paid, the second hit coming, a button cooling. Per body, so crowds ask more than a
lone hulk. **It needs a visible
tell or it reads as the hand failing at random.** A poised enemy's core rim burns hard ember for the 3 s,
and its next windup ring is drawn doubled: "the hand can't take this one." Same rule for the eye. **My pick.**

**(c) Verifier, prime plus a half restart, and the quiet heals 1/4.** The direction is right: riders
should feed the push, not sell it. But "a push of a primed button restarts half its cooldown" is
arithmetic that lands 3 s later as a sliver of ring, and the thumb never feels it. Claude's "next push costs
1" reads better (one pip, not two) but is still a discount noticed afterwards. The quiet at 1/4 is invisible in a fight and felt only at the
chime. It punishes carelessness more than it asks for a push, and blurs "HP is the fight, strain is
the run." Keep it as a dial for later, **never in the same trial as poise** (one
combat change at a time).

## Riders

The balancer and I agree: **a pushed break fires the rider**, never on the button just pushed. Hold,
shatter, the button beside it snaps full: felt at once, and bought by the push. Persuaded by the verifier: **remove Patient Lens's rider**, since it gives away the
card's own push. I disagree with his M2 as a static ban on `ready`: a ready that a push paid for
is the push's reward, not a substitute for it.
