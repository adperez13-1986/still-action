# Round 2: Claude

## What persuaded me

- **Rule 7 is the one that matters, and I had it backwards.** I kept it "narrowed to sustain". All
  three found the same thing: the autos break it. The hand does 10 and cancels the slam; the eye
  does 8, cancels the shot and halves blows. 43 + 9 free breaks in his last run against 10 pushes,
  boss pushes 3.4 -> 1.0 once the hand arrived, 0 Stopped since. Observation 1 is mostly this, not
  the riders. **Changed: keep and extend to anything free** (the translator's wording).
- **Rule 2:** the balancer's and translator's "a price the player paid" is sharper than my "creates
  a reason to spend". Taken. The verifier's M2 (a static check that no rider hands out `ready` or
  `charge`) is the enforcement, so the rule can't be argued round again.
- **Rule 3:** all four land on the same shape: one multiplier a hit, from an enemy state, cap x2.
  I'd said "two may multiply"; one is cleaner and Signal Flare already fits it. Changed.
- **Rule 4:** the translator's "changes where you stand or when you press" beats mine. Taken.
- **Rule 6:** the verifier's wording (read at >=70% of >=6 offers, maths or clarity, gifts and
  golds excluded). Anvil is a boss gift, so Mirror Ward (9/12) is the only live flag.
- **The source check** changes the frame: still-merge's rule had "or tie it to an enemy-gated
  trigger", and its x9 was fixed with exclusivity, not a ban. LESSONS made both stricter than
  their sources. And the missing lesson (the late game eased against a fixed enemy curve) is his
  observation 1.

## The three fixes for the free autos

- **(a) Autos stop breaking.** Against. On 27 Sep he said he "didn't feel" the hand; breaking
  windups is what made him feel it (pass 2), and he kept it. Taking it away regresses the one thing
  he confirmed, and risks kiting coming back.
- **(b) Poise, 3 s per enemy.** For. The first break on each enemy still lands and still feels like
  the hand; the second windup inside 3 s is his to answer (step, push, a part). One constant, and
  the break count per enemy is loggable.
- **(c) Prime + quiet 1/4.** Prime is right in spirit and hard to feel ("half cooldown" isn't
  visible on a 6-inch screen). The quiet heal is a good lever, but it's an economy change, and
  pedestals are the economy on trial. Second.

**Riders:** fire on a **pushed** break (balancer and translator), never on the button just pushed,
4 s cap. The loop he'd feel: hold a cooling button, it breaks the windup, the one beside it snaps
full. Patient Lens's rider goes (the verifier's proof: it gives away its own push).

## Enemies (observation 2)

Three proposals, all fine; the order matters. **Counter-moves first** (the translator's: the hulk
lunges at a Still who holds the band ~1.5 s; the sentinel steps behind cover from a planted Still):
they touch the two most common enemies, answer "nothing answers your answer" directly, and need no
new body. Then **the mender** (already pitched) as the first pairing. The balancer's caller is the
same idea as the mender with less fiction; drop it.
