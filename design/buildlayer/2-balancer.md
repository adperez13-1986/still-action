damage and flatten temper. That recovers about half the gap.

## 5. What the sim can't price, and what can

Each geometry piece comes down to one rate. Measure the rate, and the sim prices the rest.

| piece | the rate | log field (per fight) | headless check |
|---|---|---|---|
| pins, slams (walls) | share of shoves or root hits that end within 1 u of a wall | `wallEnds / shoves`, `pins` set, paid, expired | Piston 2 u off a wall, 20 casts (the verifier's K-B2) |
| haul, clumps | bodies an area part hits, with and without a pull first | `bodiesPerCast` per part; `caughtHauled` | 4 hulks in an open room: Cleaver alone vs Backdraft then Cleaver |
| shapers (move marked bodies) | marked bodies in a spender's reach at cast time | `markedInReach` at each spend | the same room, with and without the shaper |
| Wake ribbon | crossings per body per second | `crossings / awake body-seconds` | a bot circling at 5.5 u/s around 4 chasers |
| Breaker catch | windups started in reach per second | `windupsInReach / fightS`, snap share | 3 hulks, Still at the pack's edge |
| drive position | share of fight time the drive connects | `plantedS`, `movingS`, `nearS`, `wallS` | - |

The sim's guesses today: walls 0.25, crossings 0.6 a second, windups 0.4. Halving crossings takes Wake's c1 from +23% to
+17%. Doubling windups takes Breaker's c3 from +3% to +11%. Those are the numbers to replace first.

## 7. Scrap (Claude): noise for now

off its spot. **Leave it out of the trial.** Later, it could be one drive's own state (a scavenger), not a layer for all.
