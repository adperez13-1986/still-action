## 4. Wake and the kiting risk

On 26 Sep the free auto did 8.1 dps from 7.6 u while walking away, so standing back was always right. Any core that
pays for moving can bring that back. My round-1 ribbon did: chasers follow, chasers cross the trail, he is paid for
retreating. Claude's Trail has the same flaw. **I withdraw it.**

The skim rules (PLACEHOLDER numbers):
1. **Wake marks only what Still passes beside.** Within 1.6 u, inside a hulk's 2.4 u slam. The mark costs risk.
2. **Beside, not behind.** It counts only when Still's motion is sideways to the body (45-135 degrees). Running
   straight away marks nothing. He never needs the angle; he learns "skim past them" in one fight.
3. **The drive does almost no damage** (the balancer's 4 a brush). Wake kills nothing on its own.
4. **Every Wake spender is close.** Nothing in its eight spends from range. I drop Cracked Lens from Wake for this.
5. Once per body per second, so circling one hulk is not a farm.

Kiting at range now gives zero marks and zero damage. To be paid, Wake has to cut through the danger and turn back.

**Kill lines in the log:** Wake runs' median distance to the nearest awake body while moving above 3.5 u means it is

## 5. What a spender looks like

The balancer: a drive leaves 6-15 marks a deep pack fight, one spender uses 2-8. So the screen is full of marks, and
the question on the thumb is "where is the most I can spend, and is my spender ready".

**On bodies:** one thin cold ring round the feet, filling in thirds (cap 3) or halves (cap 2). Not pips: a mite is too
small for three dots at 0.7 zoom. Ram's ring cracks. Wake's ring frosts.

**On the button:**
- A **number** in the rim: marks this cast would spend if it fired now (0-9). Dim at 0, cold-lit at 1+.
- At 3 or more and ready, the rim pulses. At 3 or more and cooling, the push cue lights. That is the push's job now:
  "the marks are there and the button isn't, spend them now" (+2 strain).
- The spender aims at the most-marked body (the balancer's rule). The number is honest about that cast.

**When it fires:** the rings drain into the hit. The sound rises a step per mark spent, so a 6-spend sounds bigger
than a 2-spend without a damage number. A kill that spills passes its rings to the next body in a short visible arc
(the shatter, kept).

**When marks fade unspent:** a faint fizzle, and the ring drains to nothing. He hears the waste. That waste is the
missing piece, made audible: "I need another spender."

**The pause readout** says it in words: "Your wake marked 41. You spent 12. Nothing spends a crowd." (PLACEHOLDER)

**On the card:** a spender says "spends slammed: +X each" (PLACEHOLDER). A part that only adds marks is not in the
trial (the balancer measured +0-4%). Shapers say what they do to where bodies stand: "pulls them together, so your
shove slams two" (PLACEHOLDER).
