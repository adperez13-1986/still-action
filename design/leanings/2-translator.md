# Round 2: the translator (converging, read on a phone)
Test for every call: at drift-in zoom on the Poco, mid-crowd, can he see what happened and what it gave him,
with no HUD element? One moment, one place, one button. Where my round-1 idea fails that harder, I drop it.
## 1. Marksman's trigger: the eye break (verifier, balancer). Pin and steady dropped.
- The hand's break mirrored: same event, same shatter, other stance. He learned half of it on pass 2. Pin added a
  stun state and steady a meter: two more things to read in a crowd. The lead's stance clock is Still-triggered
  (rule 2), and a charge you can't see rising is invisible on a phone.
- Kept from my pitch, as looks only: **cold shatter** for eye breaks vs the hand's **ember**, and the eye's floor
  mark (three cold ticks at settle) so the brace shows before the blow. Neither adds a rule.
- Targeting: a breakable windup in the eye's reach goes first. The sightline jumping to the swelling lens is the warning.
## 2. The eye's base number: 8 whenever planted (lead). No distance gate, no ramp.
- Nothing on the floor says 5 u, so a gated shot hits for 5 or 8 and reads as noise; my ramp had the same flaw on a
  timer. In reach the hand wins the auto anyway, so 8 vs 5 only differs at 2.6-5 u. If the sim says that band is
  too generous, show it (sightline dashed under 5 u), don't hide it. The eye break has no distance gate either.
## 3. Close's head gap: retune Flare (verifier).
- Zero new parts; a lob landing on the body the hand is hitting reads close at a glance. Range 11 -> 6, travel
  800 -> 450 ms, card "Lobs a burst onto the enemy you're fighting, over its packmates." Glare (clear flash-cone
  read) waits in reserve. Welding Eye reads as "more hand": dropped. Signal Flare's mark-on-break is a hidden x2.
## 4. Marksman's arms: Piston's numbers (balancer), Clamp Toss's at-rest fix. Kickstand dropped.
- A 1.6 u shove is barely visible; at 4.0 u the body flies, and that is the marksman read. Flat numbers, no "if
  planted" clause (conditionals are invisible). Card: "A hard, narrow punch that knocks one enemy far back."
- Planted, the stick is at rest, so Toss's "the way you're steering" means nothing: throw straight away.
- Kickstand is a prop, a circle and a rule for a gap two number changes close.
## 5. Payoffs: riders on existing blues, four in the first trial.
- **Parry Clamp, Backdraft Vent** (hand break); **Patient Lens, Mirror Ward** (eye break).
- Riders **ready the button fully** (Patient: fully charged), icd-capped; no "-2 s" cuts. A 2 s cut is a sliver of
  the ring nobody sees mid-fight; a button snapping full, flashing ember or cold with a click, lands under the thumb.
- Grindstone and Flywheel wait: new parts, new cooldown rules, and Flywheel fires without a press.
## 6. Bosses: first hit on each opening counts as a break (balancer), and it must look like one.
- He already watches for openings (Assembler on a wall, the Arbiter's vent). Play the shatter and break sound on
  that hit, hand or eye, or riders fire "for no reason". One per opening, icds apply.
## 7. The pedestal match: off until 3 plain-pedestal runs (verifier).
- Two unplayed economy changes stacked make his feedback unreadable. Glyphs and riders ship now (learned while
  inert); the match switches on after, with nothing tagged worn giving one close, one marksman, one free.
## 8. Card wording and glyphs, final
- Rider parts only, bold, first: **Hand break:** / **Eye break:**, then the gift ("ready again", "fully charged").
  "Hand" names the auto, so Parry's own break doesn't read as a trigger. If the game never shows "hand"/"eye",

- Glyphs from the floor marks: close = an arc of the hand's ring; marksman = a dashed line ending in a dot. Not a

- Compare: four-slot glyph strip now -> after, plus "You'd give up: Hand break: ready again." when a rider goes back.
- Tag test: guess the glyph from the card line. Pressure Vent "shoves enemies away" reads marksman; it's a start white.
## Final design
- One tag per part (close, marksman, none); tags only steer and label. No set bonuses. Hand and eye never change.
- Two enemy-caused triggers: hand break (exists) and eye break (a planted shot landing on a breakable windup).
- Eye breaks shatter cold, hand breaks ember; the eye gets a floor mark at settle so the brace reads.
- The eye hits for 8 whenever planted; no gate, no ramp. A breakable windup in reach is its first target.
- Close head: Flare retuned to close (6 u, 450 ms, new line). Glare in reserve.
- Marksman arms: Piston 20 dmg / 4.0 u shove; Clamp Toss throws straight away when planted.
- Four riders: Parry Clamp, Backdraft (hand break); Patient Lens, Mirror Ward (eye break); each readies its own
  button fully, icd-capped, flashing ember or cold.
- Bosses: the first hit on each opening is a break, with its shatter and sound.
- Cards: **Hand break:** / **Eye break:** lead-ins; ring-arc and dashed-dot glyphs on card, compare, plinth, wall.
- Pedestal match off until 3 plain-pedestal runs; then one in three from your lean, nothing worn = one of each.
- Log eyeBreaks, rider fires per id, plantedS; pass line: eye breaks 0.5-1.5x hand breaks in marksman runs.
