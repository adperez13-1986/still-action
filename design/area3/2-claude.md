# Round 2: Claude

## Where I changed my mind

- **The alternate, not my branch.** The balancer's point settles it: the warm
  beam at the Assembler is the run's one real decision, and a second cold beam
  next to it is a menu with no cost that dilutes it. It also breaks the code's
  "never a third" beam and Grace's lean toward the exit. Alternating keeps the
  26 minutes and gives the two second halves different jobs (area II the HP
  road, area III the strain road). The verifier's "some days he goes home the
  other way" is the line for it. If the owner wants the choice later, the
  verifier says it's half an evening on the same `route` field.
- **The yard over the glasshouse.** Three of four voices found the yard alone,
  and the translator had it as runner-up. The glasshouse was mine and it's
  mostly a look.

## The boss: one train boss, from four

The four are the same idea: a machine on rails you beat by routing it, not by
dodging a body. I'd merge:
- **Body and loop** from the balancer's Engine: it runs a fixed loop in the
  yard arena, unlit rail is always safe, and the whistle is its wind-up
  (950 ms, per the balancer's slack check).
- **The lever** from the verifier's Shunter and my Switchman: points levers at
  the arena edge; a hit throws one; thrown in the lit window (1.2-1.4 s), it
  sends the engine into a buffer stop and opens it. That window is shorter than
  most cooldowns, so it's the push ask, and with strain step 1 in, a pushed hit
  on the lever is the natural answer.
- **The boss bar as a departure board** from the translator: which lever, and
  when, in the game's own type. It reads on a phone without looking away from
  the arena for long.
- Phase 2: two lit tracks, and it switches one lever back if you're slow (mine).

## The monster set

Pick four:
1. **The Signalman** (verifier, and my flagman): calls a train early on its
   track with a 1 s lamp wind-up. The area's lesson for "a pushed hit breaks
   the wind-up". First non-metal body (creosoted timber).
2. **The Handcar** (balancer): a ram on rails; it always stuns at the buffer.
   The ram's lesson made legible: rails show where it will go.
3. **Sleepers** (verifier/balancer, my ballast crawlers): mites asleep under the
   ballast, shown as a shiver in the gravel. Cheap. They must avoid lit lanes,
   or one train deletes a swarm (verifier's check).
4. **The coupled pair or the Linesman** (mine / balancer's): the one new
   archetype, a live line between two bodies. The balancer's Linesman is the
   same idea with numbers; take theirs, main rooms only.

## The bell

The translator's bell stroke (hits only what moves) is the best single idea in
round 1, but it's a second level-wide mechanic on top of trains in ten minutes
of crawl. I'd keep it for the station at depth 5 only, if at all, or save it
whole for a fourth area. The trains are enough.

## Bundle

The alternate + the yard (sidings at depth 4, the station at depth 5) + the
merged train boss + Signalman, Handcar, Sleepers, Linesman. Build the road with
the Arbiter as its boss first (the verifier's v0.2), then the new boss.
