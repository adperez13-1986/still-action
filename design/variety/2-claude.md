# Round 2: Claude

## Where I changed my mind

- **Not "the auto follows the arms part", yet.** Three voices found a simpler rule that does
  the job: the auto changes with distance, not with the part. Per-part autos can come later
  as flavour on top, if the base rule lands. The verifier's numbers settle why it matters:
  the auto (8.1 DPS at 7.6 u) out-damages every part but Piston, so close range pays nothing.
- **Leanings as behaviours can wait.** Once the auto rewards distance choices, the named
  archetypes may appear on their own. Start with tags on cards and on the wall (the verifier:
  turning parts to the wall becomes steering). Add set behaviours only if builds still blur.

## The one rule: hand and eye

The balancer's version, with the translator's legibility and the verifier's sequencing:
- **Hand:** within ~3.3 u of its target the auto becomes a close swing that hits twice as
  hard, inside the band a hulk's 2.4 u slam can't reach. Brawling kills a hulk in about half
  the time kiting does.
- **Eye:** standing still for 0.5 s, the auto reaches 11 u and picks shooters and supports
  first. The marksman is the one who plants their feet.
- **Walking at mid range** keeps today's auto. Kiting stays possible; it just stops being
  the best answer everywhere.
- **Show the pick** (translator): a faint cold ring for the hand's reach and a thin line to
  the eye's target, so where you stand is visibly your aim.
- A close break costing +1 strain (balancer) is worth trying with it.

## Enemies
- **The thief runs for the elite's drop** (translator and verifier converged): a certain
  first meeting, no new loot; plus the balancer's depths 1, 2, 4, 5.
- **Families first** (a name, a look and one twist each, from the old roster), then **one
  support** (the mender / Echo Shell that rebuilds a fallen body), then **stacked mods**, after
  fixing the verifier's two bugs (the Plated Warden overwrite, and the mod draw on the main
  random stream moving every shrine).
- The balancer's enemy-worn parts (an elite wearing one of your parts, dropping it when it
  dies) is the most Still idea in the round: keep it for the stacked-mod step.

## Bundle
Hand and eye + show the pick, then the thief, then families. Play between each. New parts
and the wall relayout after those, once we see which archetype feels thin.
