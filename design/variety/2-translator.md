# Round 2: Translator (feel, thumbs, phone-scale reads)

## Where I changed my mind

- **The close auto: I drop my 0.31 s jab and take the verifier's swing.** The jab doubles
  the auto's sound and sparks right where the 3-windup-voice cap already bites, and it
  changes `AUTO_INTERVAL`, the tempo of everything. A swing keeps the tempo and changes
  the *shape*: a small cold sweep you can see and hear is different from the lens pulse.
- **Revive becomes mend** (the verifier): a rebuilt wreck breaks "a quiet needs a kill".
- **Bulwark becomes the verifier's Mantled mod**, which fits any body.
- **The balancer's worn-part mods are the best read at phone scale in the round.** The
  body feature *is* the part's own model on the elite's back, one the player already
  knows from the wall. I take them over my Mirrored and Kindled (Molten covers Kindled).
- **Masher parts wait.** On a 1.1-1.2 s cooldown a masher keeps pressing a cooling button, and with
  a 0.5 s median press every hold becomes a push. Read Coil's accidental pushes in his measured
  runs first. If they're high, my string rule (the button stays *ready*) is the fix.

**How it becomes legible:** the rim circle from my B1 is drawn at the *swing's* reach,
always on while something is awake, brightening when the arms part is ready. A winding
hulk's ember disc (2.4 u) sits inside it. The gap between ember and cold is the band, about
0.8 u (13 px at the widest zoom): a nudge, not a run. If 13 px doesn't read on the Poco,
the circle thickens with zoom before the number moves.

**The balancer's eye: keep it, one step later, as the targeting rule.** The eye is still
for 0.5 s, reaches 11 u and aims at shooters, supports and leaders first. Merge it with
the verifier's spotter rule so there's a single sentence: *walking, everything aims at
the nearest; standing still, the auto and the head aim at the back line.* The stick
becomes target choice, which is the one choice a phone marksman lacks. The sightline
shows it: stop, and the dashed line jumps from the hulk to the sentinel behind it. Still's
lens stalk lifts a few degrees when the eye settles, so you can see the stance on his body
too. It needs a stick dead zone that ignores a resting thumb's drift. Both go in **after
his three measured runs** (the verifier's point), because they change every fight.

## (b) Leanings: tags only, no set bonuses

Cut Claude's "two of a leaning wakes a behaviour". Those are set bonuses, one is already a
number ("shortens your other cooldowns a little"), and they teach passing a good part for a
matching tag. Its behaviours live better as parts or rules (the steadied pierce is the eye).

The player doesn't need a named archetype to play. They need to *notice* they built one.
So the name appears only where you look back, never on the HUD:
- **card and wall plaque:** one glyph (close, far, around, lent). Turning parts to the wall becomes the steering wheel.
- **compare pause:** four glyphs in a row; three alike draw "leaning close".
- **corkboard card:** the caption gains the word ("Home · depth 6 · close").

## (c) The first three new parts

1. **Grindstone** (arms, the balancer's): "Refills only while an enemy is in reach, twice
   as fast." The button *is* the rim indicator: the sweep runs fast in the band and stops
   when you back off. It has an arms peg free. First.
2. **The planted lens** (head; mine, the balancer's and the verifier's all converged): the
   thesis as a button. Still's head shows the bare frame and his auto goes quiet while it
   stands. It needs the wall relayout (head is 8/8).
3. **The Winch** (legs, mine): the brawler's answer to a sentinel keeping distance, aimed
   by the stick. After the same relayout (legs 8/8).

## (d) The thief: runs for the elite's drop

Keep the elite's-drop rule (mine and the verifier's) over spawning with a part in its cage. Both are
payout-neutral, but only one makes a *moment*: the elite falls, its blue spins out, a
barrel stands up, and a hulk is still on you. A thief that spawns carrying is a chase for
any time, so it's a chore. Take the balancer's reach (depths 1, 2, 4, 5 at ~0.35, certain the
first time), the chime on each listen, and the cold chevron on the top half of the screen only.

## (e) Reading them at phone scale

- **Variants:** one prop on the top or front of the body (the camera looks down at 38°),
  nameable at 16 px/u with the core dark. First four: Iron Crawler (cone), Thermal Scanner
  (double shot), Static Frame (second ring), Furnace Tick.
- **Stacked mods:** every mod that changes the answer gets a body feature (worn parts: the
  part model, lit ember). At most one numeric mod (Quick or Plated). The nameplate is one line,
  name plus a sigil per mod; the words show for 2 s at the wake.
- **Supports:** a mender's cable is a dark line on the floor, not ember (no threat to Still). Three at most.
- **Banners:** the door names area I's roster beside the hook; the exit beam names the
  next level's (balancer C2); the depth banner names the unique ("Somewhere here: the Vault Keeper").
