# Round 2: Claude

## What round 1 settled
All four voices: systems for depth, content as the multiplier. The balancer's simulation
turns it into numbers: a build you aim for forms in 28% of runs today, 74% with picks at the
big moments; systems buy ~1.1 h of distinct play per evening against content's ~0.2 h, and
content only earns its keep once builds can be steered. So the order is steering first, then
content that multiplies it.

## Positions
- **Pick one of three: on pedestals you walk into, after each boss and at Plenty.** The
  translator is right that choices on a phone should be walked into, and that a pick at every
  drop cheapens it. Bosses and Plenty give ~3 big picks a run. Add elites only if the
  numbers stay low.
- **Fill chance down to ~0.35**, so the fourth button lights near the Assembler and the run
  has an arc from sparse to whole.
- **Tag steering later, lightly.** It needs the tags first, and picks may be enough.
- **"Push the drop" is the best idea of the round and needs Adrian's word.** Wanting a better
  part costs strain, and Stopped becomes the result of wanting too much: that's the game's
  thesis on the strain bar. It is a spend of a run resource, which brushes "no currency", so
  it should be his call, and behind a switch if built.
- **Reasons to start the next run: both.** The hook remembered (the next boss wears it) is a
  mechanical reason and cheap; wishes on the corkboard are the heart and make every new area
  worth building. Whether the kids draw the wishes themselves is Adrian's alone.
- **Infrastructure: only what the next steps need.** A run log that records offers, picks and
  drops, exportable from the phone. Save v4 when the next per-part field needs it. Seeded days
  later.
- **The verifier's warning is the most important line in the round:** Adrian's phone time is
  the budget, and the hand and the eye are both new and on. Build two or three things, then he
  plays before anything else is designed. This should be the last pitch round for a while.
- **Families wait** until picks exist: enemies that ask for answers only matter once you can
  steer a build to answer them.

## First moves
1. Pedestal picks after bosses and at Plenty, the lower fill chance, and the run log.
2. The hook remembered.
3. Then Adrian plays. Wishes, push-the-drop and families after, by his call.
