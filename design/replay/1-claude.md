# Round 1: Claude

**Direction: choices for depth, content for meaning.**

1. A new system multiplies with everything already in the game; a new area adds one area.
   With one person and agents, systems scale and content doesn't (the Line took an evening of
   agents and is stage A of four).
2. "Every run is different, and you showed up" is a run-based promise, not a campaign.
3. The gap is decisions per run, not areas: take or leave a drop, shrines, the warm beam; the
   build is complete by depth 2; ~2.6 h of distinct play.

Content stays as texture and heart: three areas, a boss per road, families, the Workshop, the
family. Enough that each run has character, not D2 volume.

**Moves, in order:**
1. Real choices at drops: pick one of three, or trade what you wear.
2. Parts that interact without stats (mark + multi-hit, pull into a nova, a push that breaks
   into the hand's strike), so leanings become builds.
3. Run-shaping decisions: the crossroads, shrines with real trade-offs, the hook, which boss.
4. Enemies that ask for answers: rosters, families and champions that favour one build.
5. An opt-in rule ladder for mastery, never bigger numbers.

**Stop:** adding areas before decisions per run are rich; stat-like passives.
