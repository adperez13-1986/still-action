# Archetypes (draft 1, 3 Oct 2026)

His ask: "we need to get back to the basic archetypes before going crazy on a certain mechanic... my frame of reference is always
D2" (close: Barbarian / Paladin / Assassin; ranged: Sorceress / Amazon; summoner: Druid / Necromancer), "our 4 parts mechanic and
roguelite gameplay is not letting us develop those archetypes". Keep the four body slots. Three archetypes. Lead's draft, medium
effort, no design agents. Every name and number is PLACEHOLDER.

## The diagnosis

A slot is a body part and every part belongs to one slot, so every build is one arm swing + one head lens + one torso blast + one leg
dash: a hybrid by construction. A core on top can't change that. D2's classes decide three things before any skill drops: what the
kit may hold, how the class survives, and where its power comes from.

## The idea: the archetype sets each slot's law

The four slots stay (head, torso, arms, legs: Still is still a robot assembling himself). The archetype, picked at the run's start
in place of today's core pick, decides **which part families each slot accepts**. Parts sort into families by shape:

| family | parts today |
|---|---|
| **Strike** (close, arc/grab/catch) | Scrap Cleaver, Piston, Rusted Hook, Parry Clamp, Frayed Cleaver, Clamp Toss, Anvil |
| **Bolt** (shots, lobs) | Focusing Lens, Flare, Cracked / Ricochet / Patient Lens, Signal Flare, Through-Line, Overclocked Coil |
| **Blast** (novas round him) | Pressure Vent, Backdraft Vent, Chill Vent |
| **Guard** | Ward, Brace, Mirror Ward |
| **Move** (dash, hop, anchor) | Kickstart, Skitter, Skid Plates, Overrun, Frost Trail, Spring Heels, Plumb Line, Borrowed Time |
| **Summon** | Lure, and new ones (§4) |

| slot | Brawler | Marksman | Summoner |
|---|---|---|---|
| head | Blast or Guard ("a roar") | **Bolt** | Bolt or **Summon** ("a command") |
| torso | Blast or Guard | Guard or Blast | **Summon** |
| arms | **Strike** | **Bolt** ("arm cannon") or Strike | **Summon** or Strike |
| legs | Move or **Strike** (charges: Overrun, Skid Plates) | Move | Move |

So a Marksman can carry two or three shooters, and a Brawler can carry two strikes and two blasts, which today's game forbids. A part
in a "borrowed" slot keeps its own numbers (an arm cannon is the lens as it is). Parts outside an archetype's law don't drop for it.

## The three

**Brawler** (Barbarian, Paladin). Close, in the middle of the pack.
- Auto: **the hand** (today's close auto).
- Survives by: **Hardened**, damage taken x0.85 (Thorns' number).
- Starts with: Scrap Cleaver (arms), Pressure Vent (torso), Kickstart (legs), Ward (head).
- Builds within it (D2's trees): heavy hitter (Piston, Anvil, charges), crowd breaker (vents, Clamp Toss into walls), guardian (Ward,
  Brace, Mirror, Parry Clamp).

**Marksman** (Amazon, Sorceress). Mid range, keeps distance.
- Auto: **the eye** (today's ranged auto).
- Survives by: **Footwork**, move speed x1.1 and Move parts recharge 25% faster.
- Starts with: Focusing Lens (head), Flare (arms, as a cannon), Skitter (legs), Ward (torso).
- Builds within it: sniper (Patient, Through-Line), spray (Coil, Ricochet, Cracked), artillery (Flare, Signal Flare, lobs over cover).

**Summoner** (Necromancer, Druid). Holds back, fights through bodies.
- Auto: **a drone**, a small companion that follows him and pecks the nearest enemy (low damage, short range from itself). The
  "same verb at two lengths" problem is gone: the three autos are three different things.
- Survives by: **Crowd**, enemies within 3 u of a summon go for the summon before him.
- Starts with: Lure (torso), the Turret (arms, new), Kickstart (legs), Focusing Lens (head).
- Builds within it: swarm (many small summons), fortress (turrets, walls), pack leader (the drone grows).

## The cores become sub-styles

Today's cores stop being the identity. After the Assembler (d3), he's offered **two sub-styles from his archetype** and takes one,
like spending into a D2 skill tree: Brawler: Ram or Thorns; Marksman: Tether or Wake; Summoner: two new ones later (e.g. the drone
evolves, or summons leave marks). That gives variation within an archetype across runs. Until d3 he plays the archetype bare.
Mark verbs (Cash / Spread / Detonate / Feed) wait: they may become one sub-style's flavour rather than a global rule.

## §4 New content (only the Summoner needs it)

- **Turret** (arms or torso, white): drops a small turret beside him that shoots the nearest enemy for ~6 s. Recast moves it.
- **Drone upgrade parts** (head, blue): the drone gets a second drone, a lunge, or a shield.
- **Scrap Hound** (arms, blue): a short-lived runner that chases and bites, then bursts.
- **Barricade** (torso, blue): a short wall of scrap where he stands; enemies path around it.
Brawler and Marksman need nothing new to start; their families exist.

## Build order (each a small Sonnet step)

1. **A1 the pick and the slot laws:** archetype pick at run start (three cards), slot law per archetype, starter kits, the three
   traits, Brawler + Marksman playable. Cores hidden from the pick. No drop changes yet (parts outside the law are simply skipped).
2. **A2 the Summoner:** the drone auto, Crowd, Turret and Lure; the Summoner card goes live.
3. **A3 the hunt:** drops weighted to the archetype's families (exact share TBD with dropsim), the other Summoner parts.
4. **A4 sub-styles:** the post-Assembler offer of two cores per archetype.

Bare game and old saves keep working: an old save resumes with no archetype (today's rules).
