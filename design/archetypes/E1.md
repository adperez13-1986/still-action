## 1. Data: `src/evolutions.ts` (no three.js)

- `EvoId = 'whirlwind' | 'rail'` (E2/E3 add more). `EVOLUTIONS: Record<EvoId, { part: PartId; partner: { part?: PartId; family?: Family }; name; line; over: Partial<AbilityDef> }>`:
  - `whirlwind`: part `scrap-cleaver`, partner family `blast`. Line `A full spin that carries you forward and hits everything around you twice.`
    Numbers: cone 360, damage x1.4 of the III cleaver per hit, 2 hits 0.12 s apart, carries Still `travel 1.5 u` along the stick
    (or facing) over ~0.25 s; reach: the cleaver's range at III.
  - `rail`: part `focusing-lens`, partner family `move`. Line `A beam through everything in a line. Every dash ends with a free shot.`
    Numbers: damage x1.6 of the III lens, pierces every body (walls stop it), and after any Move-family part's cast ends, a free
    Rail shot fires at the nearest enemy in range (no cooldown cost, at most once per dash).
- `evoFor(partId, wornParts, arch)`: the evolution whose partner is worn in ANOTHER slot (use archetypes.ts FAMILY), else null.
- Only under an archetype. With no archetype nothing here is read.

## 2. The trigger and the state

- `run.evolved: PartId[]` (or a set keyed by worn slot) in the snapshot, no version bump; old saves have none.
- Check after a melt (rank change), a take, and a resume: a worn part at rank III with its partner worn in another slot, not yet
  evolved -> evolve. Permanent for the run (stays if the partner leaves; a swap away of the evolved part itself loses it).
- An evolved part's def = the III def with `over` applied (like cores.ts `variant` / `asWorn`; find where asWorn is applied in
  main.ts and hook there). Rank stays III; melting into it does nothing (the melt card says `already evolved`).
- **Masteries:** under an archetype, never offered (grep `masteryOffer` in main.ts). A melt past III into a part with no evolution
  says `nothing more to learn yet` (WORDS, PLACEHOLDER). No archetype: masteries exactly as today.

## 3. Showing it

- **The card** of every part that has an evolution, from rank I, on floor cards, take cards and pause cards: a line
  `evolves with: any blast` / `evolves with: a move part` (family names in plain words: blast, strike, shot, guard, move, summon).
- **Pause screen:** for each worn part with an evolution, `Whirlwind: rank II of III, partner worn` or `... partner missing`.
- **The moment:** world pauses on a card: title `Scrap Cleaver evolves`, the new name big, its line, old icon -> new icon, one key
  `on`. A cold flash and shard burst on Still, a rising chime (new sfx, synthesised like the others).
- **The button:** new icon (a spin glyph for Whirlwind, a long beam glyph for Rail) and a bright cold rim that stays.

## 4. The two looks (the point of evolutions: a different animation, not bigger)

- **Whirlwind:** Still spins a full turn per hit (two turns) while carried forward, a cold ring trail around him at the cleaver's
  reach, sparks on each body hit. Use the existing pose system (still.ts `attack`, beats) for a new `spin` beat.
- **Rail:** a long straight cold beam from his head to the wall or range end, bright core + soft edge, lingering 0.3 s and fading,
  a flash at each body it passes. Reuse partfx.beam if it fits. The free shot after a dash fires from where the dash ends.
