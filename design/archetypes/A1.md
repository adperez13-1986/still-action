## 3. The slot law (the core of this step)

Today a part's slot is `def.slot`. With an archetype, a part may be worn in **any slot whose law accepts its family**; the slot it's
worn in is the "worn slot", which may differ from `def.slot`. Find every place that keys on `def.slot` for equipping, offering, the
HUD buttons, the pause cards, drops and the save (grep `\.slot\b` in main.ts, pause.ts, hud.ts, loot.ts, drops.ts, pool.ts, save.ts,
combat.ts). Fire, aim and pose must follow the part's shape, not its slot. Where a check is ambiguous, use the worn slot for
"which button" and the part's own def for "what it does".
- **Taking a floor part:** with an archetype, a part goes to its own `def.slot` if the law allows it there; otherwise to the first
  allowed slot. If it can go in two slots (a lens for a Marksman: head or arms), the take card offers both ("head" / "arms",
  showing what each replaces).
- **Offers and drops:** a part with no allowed slot under the archetype is never offered or dropped (re-roll it; no weighting yet).
- With no archetype, everything is exactly today's.
