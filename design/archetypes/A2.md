# A2: the Summoner

From `ARCHETYPES.md`. His call (3 Oct): strikes stay allowed in the Brawler's legs. This step: the Summoner card goes live with
its drone auto, the Crowd trait, the new Turret part and its kit. Drone upgrades, Scrap Hound and Barricade wait (A3).
Token budget is tight: grep, read only what's named, and run the full suite once at the end. Numbers and words go in
`archetypes.ts` and are PLACEHOLDER.

## 1. The drone (the Summoner's auto, in place of the hand / eye)

- A small cold drone follows Still: it hovers at `follow 1.3 u` behind and to his side and eases there (`ease 6 /s`), ~1.2 u up.
- Every `everyS 0.7` s it pecks the nearest awake, targetable enemy within `range 4.5 u` **of the drone** (walls block, like
  shots): a short cold bolt from the drone, `damage` = 70% of the eye auto's per-hit damage (read the eye's constant; set it as
  a ratio `dmgOfEye 0.7`), through the auto path, as form `'drone'`.
- Enemies ignore the drone (it isn't a body). It never dies. Hidden with no archetype or another archetype.
- Look: one small mesh (a cold diamond with a faint ring), bobbing. Its bolt reuses the eye's bolt look, smaller.

## 2. Crowd (the Summoner's trait)

- An awake enemy within `radius 3 u` of a live summon (a Turret, or Lure's decoy) targets that summon instead of Still.
  Lure's decoy already pulls enemies (`targetFor`, `parts.decoy`): extend that same path to turrets and to the Crowd radius.
  Without the Summoner, Lure works as today.

## 3. The Turret (new part, white, family `summon`, home slot arms)

- Press: drops a turret at Still's feet (or `offset 1.0 u` toward the stick). It lives `lifeS 6` s or until it takes `hp 30`
  damage from enemies (melee on it via the decoy path; shots hit it if they reach it). Every `everyS 0.5` s it fires at the
  nearest awake enemy within `range 7 u` (walls block): `damage 4`, as a part hit from its slot (so it flows through hitPart like
  any part). Cooldown `cooldownMs 7000`. A recast while one stands moves it (the old one pops).
- One turret at a time.
- Look: a small squat cold tripod with a barrel that turns to its target, a thin life ring at its base that drains. Two meshes at
  most. A pop of shards when it dies or expires.
- Card line: `Drops a turret that shoots the nearest enemy.`
- **The Turret must never appear without the Summoner:** not in today's pool, offers, drops, the workshop or partsview while no
  archetype is worn, and not for Brawler or Marksman (their laws reject `summon` already: check). With no archetype the game is
  exactly today's.

## 4. The Summoner card and kit

- `ARCH_LIVE` gains `summoner`; its card reads its line and `Others fight for you.` / survival `Enemies near your summons go for them.`
- Kit: head Focusing Lens, torso Lure, arms Turret, legs Kickstart.
