# Evolutions (draft 1, 3 Oct 2026)

His questions: masteries "who are they for? ... I don't see their effect... can they be both more dramatic and powerful? When I
think of masteries I think of Vampire Survivors where the attack animation and strength become totally different." Lead's answer:
masteries were built on purpose to change what an auto does, not how hard (28 Sep), and most only set a state that pays only if a
"lands twice" part is worn: invisible. With archetypes there is one auto each (none for the Summoner's drone). And the shallowest part
of the game is that **parts barely interact**. Evolutions replace masteries under archetypes. Lead's draft, no design agents. Every
name and number is PLACEHOLDER.

## The rule

- A part evolves when it is **at rank III** and its **partner is worn in another slot** (a named part, or any part of a family).
  Whichever comes second triggers it.
- The moment is loud: the world pauses on a card ("Scrap Cleaver evolves: Whirlwind", old icon -> new, its new line), a flash on
  Still, a new icon on its button with a bright cold rim. It's permanent for the run, even if the partner leaves later.
- **Visible from the start:** every part's card shows `evolves with: <partner>` from rank I, and a worn part whose partner is
  worn shows the pair on the pause screen ("Whirlwind: rank II of III"). The puzzle is in plain sight (his legibility rule).
- A part with two possible evolutions (Focusing Lens) evolves into the first completed; if both complete at once, a choice card.
- An evolved part has new numbers, a new shape or behaviour, and a new animation. Rank no longer applies to it (it is past III).
- **Masteries:** off under an archetype (melting past III into a part with no evolution yet does nothing new; the card says so).
  With no archetype the game is today's, masteries included.

## The first six (two per archetype)

| part (III) | + partner | evolves into | what changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scrap Cleaver** | any **blast** | **Whirlwind** | a full 360° spin that carries him 1.5 u along the stick, hitting everything around twice (x1.4 each). New animation: the spin, with a cold ring trail. D2's Barbarian. |
| **Pressure Vent** | **Ward** | **Bastion** | the blast also raises a shield that blocks his next 2 hits for 4 s; each blocked hit fires a small free vent (shove 2 u, 8 dmg). Blast + guard: the Paladin. |
| **Focusing Lens** | any **move** part | **Rail** | the bolt becomes a beam that pierces every body in a line (x1.6), and each dash ends with a free Rail shot at the nearest enemy. A long cold line that lingers 0.3 s. Bow and footwork. |
| **Flare** | a second **bolt** | **Barrage** | the lob splits into 5 bomblets in a ring at the target (0.6x each, radius 1.2), a cluster you see land one after another. Artillery. |
| **Turret** | **Lure** | **Twin Sentry** | two turrets at once, living 9 s, joined by a cold beam: a body that crosses it takes 6 (once per 0.5 s). Summons that make a place. |
| **Focusing Lens** | **Turret** | **Spotter** | the lens's target is spotted for 4 s: a cold reticle over it, and the drone and turrets all fire at it for x1.5. The Summoner's focus. |

Brawler: Whirlwind, Bastion. Marksman: Rail, Barrage. Summoner: Twin Sentry, Spotter. Every partner is a part or family an
archetype can actually wear together (check against the slot laws).
**The Turret must drop for the Summoner** (today it's kit-only, so it could never reach III): it joins the Summoner's drops only.

## Build order

- **E1:** the framework (trigger, card, button rim, `evolves with` lines, pause pairs, masteries off under an archetype) +
  **Whirlwind** and **Rail**, the two most common kit parts (Brawler, Marksman). **Stop for his feel**: dramatic enough? powerful enough?
- **E2:** Bastion, Barrage.
- **E3:** Turret in the Summoner's drops; Twin Sentry, Spotter.
- Later: more evolutions (every part should have one eventually), and A3 / A4 as planned.
