# Round 2: Claude

**The 67% was mine and it was wrong** (balancer, verifier). On the 2 runs that log both sides the autos did
64% when he never melted and 34% when he invested. So "the autos carry" is true for the player who doesn't
grow, and for everyone at depths 1-2. That makes the problem narrower than I wrote it, not gone: the
never-melt run is the one where a press is worth two free beats.

**Changed: I drop my time window for the verifier's claim.** The translator and verifier named the failure
better than I did: not "the autos do too much" but "a body he never chose dies to a beat he never pressed".
My window, and the balancer's bank too, earn beats that can still land on any body in reach. They make him
press more without making the presses decide the kills. The claim ties each auto to a body a part touched,
so an arc or a nova claims a crowd, a lens claims the back line, and an ignored hulk stays whole. It also
keeps every settled thing: autos still set states, mastery still has a home, the brace stays.

**Where I disagree with the balancer:** D3 is the only option with numbers, and that's worth a lot, but its
headline is that it leaves the median and investor players *untouched*. If the thing he feels is who kills,
a change that doesn't touch two of the three players hasn't reached it. I'd want the claim modelled before
choosing (I've asked for it).

**Where I disagree with the translator:** four changes at once (no planted shot, strike at 5, no breaks,
x2 after a part) is a new combat system, not a trial. The balancer's A1 row says halving the autos alone
takes the never-melt finish rate from 31% to 6%. Heading-aim is a good idea on its own, and it costs no
thumb, but it belongs in a second pass. The one part of the package I'd keep in the trial is **the planted
shot**: if the claim still feels like Archero while standing still, the lance is the next thing to cut
(the verifier says the same).

**Where I'd still push back on him:** a fifth pressed button (C) got no support from anyone, for reasons
that hold: on a phone it gets held, and a held basic is an auto that costs the only thumb that presses
parts. If what he wants is Hades, the translator's point stands: Still already has Hades's four verbs in
its four slots. The fix is making them carry the fight, not adding a fifth.

**Trial I'd take to the phone:** one pause switch, "claimed autos" (words his), built like "parry catch",
off by default. With the verifier's log fixes first (`autoDmgReal`, kills by part vs auto, `idleS`), so the
run can actually be judged.
