# Round 2: Balancer

## (a) The three I'd back most

1. **The warm beam** (translator R2, verifier R2 and its one-evening build).
   It adds a real decision at a moment the run already has, when the
   Assembler falls. It gives the run an end without a score, and Made it
   becomes reachable from run 1. It's cheap, and it needs no save.
2. **The pool opens from rare sources** (verifier M1). The boss gold is always
   unseen, and elites and Plenty are 25% unseen. That's ~1.5-2 finds a run and
   a full pool in 10-14 runs, the same pace as my M1. It's also one filter.
   Plenty becomes the loop's best trade: +4 strain buys a chance at width.
3. **The shelf, or turn it to the wall** (verifier M3, translator M8). This is
   a cheaper fix for dilution than my crate. A player who shelves down to 12
   non-whites sees any one part in 92% of runs and a pair in 85%, however big
   the catalog grows. The floor of 2 parts per slot keeps it from collapsing.

## (b) What breaks

- **Unlocks keyed to run count** (translator rule 1). If the *pool* opens on
  run count, the fastest run is the best farm: break on depth 1 thirty times.
  Run count is fine for things with no mechanics (rugs, drawings, the kids).
  Pool unlocks have to come from playing (bosses, elites, Plenty).
- **The shell "counts as one crate"** (verifier R4). That's 0.1 of a part and
  +6 HP of expected value, placed at exactly the depth you struggled. It's
  small, but it's a later run being stronger. Make it give nothing.
- **Depth 12 soft cap** (verifier R2). That's 4 Assemblers and ~55 min, too
  long for a phone and the same boss four times. Cap at 9.
- **The warm beam when Broken keeps everything.** Going deeper then
  dominates while the pool is filling: more finds, and no downside. That's
  acceptable, because it only lasts ~10 runs, but it means "home" is a feel
  choice, not a maths one, until the wall is full. Say so rather than fake a
  cost.
- **My own "Broken loses this depth's parts"**. See (c). Withdrawn.
- **My R6 (the Assembler wears your discards)**. It costs a boss-side
  telegraph per part. Withdrawn.

## (c) Positions

**Kids.** The translator is right: gating them on runs makes love conditional
on attendance. They're present from the first night home. Their parts' drop
rules stay the owner's. I have no maths reason to gate them.

**Run length.** I recommend **three areas of two crawls plus the Assembler (9
depths), then the walk into the light**. The warm beam comes after Assembler
II; after III, it's the only door. I'd leave it out after Assembler I: at
depth 3 little has been found yet, and a 13-minute Made it becomes the safe
loop. Save at every beam (translator R1) so 40 minutes survives a phone.

| home at | minutes | blue drops | P(named blue), 16 pooled |
|---|---|---|---|
| depth 6 | ~26 | ~22 | 75% |
| depth 9, then the walk | ~41 | ~30 | 85% |

**Wear vs the quiet room.** These solve different halves. The quiet room is
a valve, and a valve is worth nothing while strain doesn't carry. Today a
level's six quiets take back −12, so six pushes a level cost nothing. The
cheapest fix is one number, not my second bar: **quiet −2 → −1**. A level then
absorbs 3 pushes, and a 5-push player gains +4 a level. The quiet room (−6 for
a whole level's ~8 parts) and Rest become the strain budget, and growth vs
comfort appears on the map by itself. Keep Wear in reserve if late depths
still don't threaten.

**Sit down, and Broken losing parts.** This fails the genesis rule, "progress
always persists, you never leave with nothing". I'm withdrawing both. Broken
keeps everything. Stopped stays strain-only, and its value is in the run: it's
how an aggressive, push-heavy run ends, and the target is 25-30% of runs.
Brace, which turns a Broken into a Stopped, is already the in-game way to
choose it.

## (d) Combined pitches

**Workshop: the warm room, put back together.** A walkable room (T-W1, V-W1).
Grace is its light, and the grade eases at the threshold (T-W2). The ending
plays backwards into reassembly on the bench (V-W3, C-W2), and while he's put
back together you pick which found white he wakes holding (T-M2). The kids
are home from the first night, first as traces just out of frame (T-W3). The
corkboard pins a card per run with the photo or their drawing (T-W4, C-W1),
and my strain line runs along the bottom edge. It's at most 2 decisions a
visit. Cost: medium.

**Meta: the pool opens, you shape it.** Twelve parts at the start, unseen
parts from the boss, elites and Plenty, full in ~10-12 runs (V-M1). The
pegboard shows it filling (T-M1), and any part turns to the wall (M8/M3).
Parts remember their runs (T-M4, V-M2). One-time moments open map node kinds
(V-M4, my M5): the first Stopped opens the quiet room, the first Assembler
the elite den, the first home the swap (T-R7). No currency.

**Run: three areas and the walk home.** Three areas of 2 crawls plus the
Assembler. The Assembler keeps its 900 HP each time but gets new adds (rams,
then mites under a Warden). Each crawl is picked at a two-beam exit with floor
glyphs (T-R4, V-R3). There's a warm beam after II. After III comes a
30-60-second walk into Grace's light (T-R3), and the areas run morning to
night (C-R1). Quiet −1 makes strain carry. All three endings go home (T-R8),
and the depth moves to the card's caption, off the headline.

## Summary

1. I back the warm beam, the pool opening from rare sources, and the shelf (it fixes dilution: 92% for one part and 85% for a pair at 12 shelved non-whites).
2. What breaks: pool unlocks keyed to run count (break early to farm), the shell as a free crate, a 12-depth cap, and my own Broken-loses-parts rule, which fails the genesis rule, so I've withdrawn it.
3. Kids home from the first night (translator wins). Run is 9 depths in three areas, with a warm beam after II and a final walk after III (~26 or ~41 min), saved at every beam.
4. Quiet −2 → −1 is the cheapest way to make strain carry. It makes the quiet room worth taking, and Wear stays in reserve. Stopped's value lives in the run, not in a penalty on Broken.
5. Combined: a warm room where Still is put back together, a pool that opens and that you shape, and three areas with a walk home.
