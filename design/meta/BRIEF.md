For the design agents. **This is a pitch round, not a spec.** The owner wants
several *simple* idea pitches for each of three topics, to react to. Nothing
gets built from this round. Keep each pitch short enough to read on a phone:
a name, 3–6 sentences, what the player does, why it fits Still, and the cost
(cheap / medium / big).

## Hard constraints (the owner's)

- **Premium, buy-once. No grind, no daily-login pressure, no currency you farm
  for hours, no stat upgrades that make a later run stronger than an earlier
  one.** Runs get wider (more options), not stronger.
- **Family is presence, not power.** Grace is the one warm light, never a unit.
  Yanah and Yuri are two rare parts that are theirs, protective rather than
  strong. **The owner writes those two parts himself: don't design their
  abilities.** Where they are and how they show up in the Workshop is fair
  game.
- **Every run ends with encouragement**, whatever the outcome. The two endings
  (Broken, Stopped) stay different on purpose.
- Rules are symmetric; threats are telegraphed; phone first (landscape,
  thumbs); cold for Still, ember for enemies, warm only for Grace.
- It's built for fun, by one person, with vibe coding. It isn't a shipping
  commitment, so don't pitch anything that needs a content treadmill.
- Don't assume the owner has played the games you cite. Describe the idea
  itself, not "like Hades's Mirror".

## The three topics

1. **The Workshop**: the space between runs. What it is, what you do there,
   what it shows, how Yanah and Yuri are present.
2. **Meta progression**: what carries from run to run, and how "wider not
   stronger" feels rewarding instead of flat.
3. **A complete run**: a run needs a shape and an end. How long it is, what
   "reaching the end" means for a game whose line is "you showed up, that was
   enough", the node map, and what makes run 10 feel different from run 1.

Pitch at least 3 ideas per topic, and at least one of them should be
surprising: something none of the old documents said.
